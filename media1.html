Media Response 1

This is a media response for the documentry Eno (2024), directed by Gary Hustwit

Producers in music are the people behind the scenes. I think that this documentry shows that as I didn't know of him before this screening. Another example of this is like his work behind creating the windows bootup sound, a famous three second intro that unless serached for isn't really publicized.

The two things that I took down notes for and I connected with was the cards and his philosophy on input and output. The cards were based on ideas in the moment and he used them to give direction. I jotted down in the moment that in a space as abstract as music that using these cards allows us and him to ground the work to something physical and visual. It gives structure and reality to music which is abstract. The idea of input and output also connected with me. It reminds me of a conversation. Only one person can talk at a time. If you are taking in information you cannot at the same time produce. This part I saw as advice on how to create balance, setting time for both.
