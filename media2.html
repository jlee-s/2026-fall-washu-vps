Media Response 2

This is a media response to There are No Gardens on the Internet, a website by Chia Amisola

I really liked the colors on the website they really drew the eyes in. I think that the concept of having each item have a quote of idea behind it was intresting. I didn't quite get the idea of no gardens on the internet, because to me it seems she is trying to argue that the internet cannot be a garden, while using the website as a garden?

I guess overall I think I was confused by the message but I think the website is really well built and feels like I am exploring someone's mind.
