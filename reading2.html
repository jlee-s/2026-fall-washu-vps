Reading Response 2

This is a reading response for the The New Turing Test by Elan Ullendorf in 2023.

I found that this article focused on the idea of LLM being used to replicate the work required by humans. The author condemns this act and argues that the way these machines have been used creates a black hole of accountability. The parts that drew me into this was the idea of creating emotional connections with the audience. I agree with this statement and believe that when we are faced with a human audience, the art of writing and design is able to draw out the emotions of the reader. However, I believe that in some cases the use of AI can actually bolster our humanness by allowing us to direct our energy on tasks that require it. For example when designing using AI to create various prototypes of your ideas helps save time and visualize. Using these you can build out your favorite design, saving you time and allowing you to put more effort into creating a better, more emotional design.

A quote that I felt conveyed the entire article was “If the original Turing test evaluated what computers are capable of, this new Turing test evaluates what we are capable of” (Ullendorff 2023). It encapsulates the idea that we shouldn’t use AI to emulate humans and that to tackle this problem we should focus on ourselves and what we can do rather than what AI can do! :)
