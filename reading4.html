Reading Response 4

This is a reading response for the The Forbidden Zone by Damon Zucconi, Charles Broskoski, and Laurel Schwulst in 2023.

The quote "I’ve always had this sense that if you build a container for something, you will make things to fill it," was a intresting idea to me. I feel that for me the opposite thing is what drives my creativity. It is when I am actively making soemthing like stickers or ceramics that creates this ambition in me to find a place for it. However, I do agree with the idea that there will always be a thing to fill the container with. It may take a while but the perfect item will come along and everything will work out.

The second thing that stood out to me reading this was the idea of a container being unproductive. I found the idea that a container is already unproductive when we don't use it. For me, I think that a container becomes unproductive when it does harm, so when it is past netural. For example, I don't delete playlists on spotify until they get in my way while looking for something or I feel that they don't represent what I am.

Overall, I felt that the reading gave good insight into how to input things from around us and interact with them in ways that make us happy.
