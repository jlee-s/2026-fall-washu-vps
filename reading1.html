Reading Response 1

This is a reading response for the conversation about restaurant websites from 2019.

I connected with this article’s point that a good menu should convey the spirit/vibe of the restaurant. It gives the reader a better sense of what they are getting themselves into. Like the examples given, when done right the menu through its shapes, colors and fonts conveys not only the vibe, but also subtle things like the price point. I really felt that when I was comparing the Dominio one with Kajitsu. The Dominio one was very blocky, everything was the same, it felt very corporate, while the Kajitsu felt more soft and free flowing.

One part that I found really interesting was the idea of restaurant websites being a facebook page. I agree that when you use another website as your template you lose control of certain aspects. However, I think that in a way better designs can arise from these because the designers are forced to look for creative ways to share their information. For example, people using multiple posts in a row to create a giant mural on their instagram page. In a way it creates a result that is unique to the restaurant and is still able to convey the idea.
