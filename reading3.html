Reading Response 3

This is a reading response for the My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be? by Laurel Schwulst in 2018.

When reading this piece, the part that stood out to me was the quote , “we need individuals rather than corporations to guide the web’s future.” I agree with this statement, and I think that if individuals had a choice almost everyone would have one. However, I think the two things that are holding back the general public are cost and experience. First, the cost to have a website online requires a monthly hosting fee or ads to be placed on the website. While the cost is often not that much, to someone who doesn’t need a website it appears pointless. Second, having the knowledge and experience with the tools needed to create a website requires time to learn. To a population that already isn’t tech savvy, understanding how to create a unique website that is able to do everything they imagine is hard.

It was also interesting to read about websites being able to embody things in nature like a puddle. I had never thought of websites like that before and by framing it in this way you can really see how it was made to allow information to pass from individual to individual and that everything else should be prioritized after that.
